## What was built

Upstream already has the whole Hor+ chain (upstream docs/SETTINGS_MENU_WIDESCREEN.md): the view-window wrapper and the
entity-plane widening (`src/game/vcs_display.cpp`), the presenter's display aspect and 4:3 frames for 2D-only pictures
(`render/presenter`, `ps2_runtime.cpp`), the GS renderer's HUD squeeze, HUD classification and wide targets
(`gs_hw.cpp`), the cutscene-bars setting and the game's own widescreen preference kept off. The Switch build links all
of it through the OpenGL backend (patch 0008) without change; M09 makes it the Switch's default and proves it there.
